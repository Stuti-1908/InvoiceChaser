    FAQ

    Questions business owners ask us.

    Will this annoy my customers?

    Reminders are polite, timed to the due date and limited in number. Any reply pauses the chase, a payment stops it, and you approve every template before anything is sent.

    What happens when a customer disputes an invoice?

    Reminders stop on that invoice and it goes to your team with the customer's reason and the full message history.

    Is this a debt collection agency?

    No. InvoiceChaser is software you use to follow up on your own invoices, in your own company's name. Difficult cases go back to your own team.

    Which accounting software do you connect to?

    QuickBooks and spreadsheet uploads today. Zoho Books, Xero and e-invoice feeds are on the way. Ask us about yours.

    How long does setup take?

    Most pilots start within a week: connect your invoices, approve the templates, choose which invoices to include.

    Where is my data kept?

    Tell us your requirements during the pilot call. We agree how customer data is stored and handled before any real data is shared.
