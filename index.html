Why it matters

Late payment is normal in UAE B2B, and it costs more than it looks.

47%

49%

Source: Atradius Payment Practices Barometer, UAE, survey conducted Q2 2026.

Every late invoice is an interest-free loan to your customer.

  • Cash is stuck while you still pay salaries, rent and suppliers.
  • Someone on your team spends hours a week sending reminders and making awkward calls.
  • Follow-ups get forgotten when things are busy, so the oldest invoices get the least attention.

How it works

Four steps, and your team only handles the exceptions.

  1. 1

    Connect

    Link your accounting software or upload a spreadsheet of invoices and customer contacts.

  2. 2

    Watch

    InvoiceChaser sees what is coming due and what is already overdue, invoice by invoice.

  3. 3

    Chase

    Polite reminders go out on WhatsApp, email and phone, in the right order, at the right time.

  4. 4

    Stop

    The moment a payment arrives, reminders stop. Replies pause the chase and reach the right person.

    FAQ

    Common questions

    Will this annoy my customers?

    Reminders are polite, timed to the due date, and limited in frequency. Any reply pauses the chase, payment stops it, and you approve every template first.

    What happens if a customer disputes an invoice?

    The chase stops on that invoice and the case goes to your team with the customer's reason and the full message history.

    Is this a debt collection agency?

    No. InvoiceChaser is software you use to follow up on your own invoices, in your own name. Unresolved cases go to your own team.

    Which accounting software do you connect to?

    QuickBooks and spreadsheet uploads today, with more connections, including Zoho Books and e-invoice feeds, on the roadmap. Ask us about yours.

    Which languages and channels?

    WhatsApp, email, SMS and phone, in English and Arabic.

    What does it cost?
