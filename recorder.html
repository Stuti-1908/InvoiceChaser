Record the website call

Use this page in Microsoft Edge to turn the sample call into audio files the website can play in every browser.

  1. Pick a voice for Sara and for Khalid in each language (names with "Online (Natural)" sound best).
  2. Press Record. When Edge asks what to share, choose This tab and turn on Also share tab audio.
  3. The call plays and records. When it finishes, click the download link.
  4. Rename the files call-en.webm and call-ar.webm and put them in the website's audio/ folder.
